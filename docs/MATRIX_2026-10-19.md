### Why the failure group is an S0 ablation

Pilot runs showed that a short budget and no-BC initialization still passed the 20-episode acceptance. The task is robust to those ablations under the scripted low-level controller, so they were not reliable negative samples.

The failure group therefore uses `--s0 zero`: the high-level observation and action interface is unchanged, but the low-level controller emits zero commands. The robot cannot make progress. This is a **controlled negative cohort**, not a natural training failure.
